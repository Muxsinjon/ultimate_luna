## 3. Real mission: Chandrayaan-3 (ISRO), 69.373 S 32.319 E

Our terrain-aware model (upper-limb rule, no tuning) predicts: **sunrise** 2023-08-21 11:20 UTC, then **sunset** 2023-09-04 09:00 UTC, then **sunrise** 2023-09-19 20:10 UTC. The Sun was 8.62° above the smooth horizon at touchdown.

Public record: Landed 2023-08-23 12:34 UTC; Vikram put to sleep ~2023-09-04 02:30 UTC ahead of local sunset (reported ~Sep 4-5); wake-up attempted from the next sunrise, announced as Sep 22.

Sources: ISRO mission updates (Sep 2023); The National, 2023-09-05, "India's Chandrayaan-3 mission lander and rover in sleep mode for lunar night"; India TV News, 2023-09-21, on the Sep 22 wake-up attempt.

## Known uncertainty

- Ephemeris interpolation (cubic, Milestone 4): Earth direction max 0.0014°, Sun max 0.0003° against the full hourly Horizons series.
- Terrain: 240 m polar grid (960 m and about 7.6 km for custom points). Near-field slopes and boulders are not resolved, so the horizon can be wrong by tenths of a degree near the site. At the pole, the Sun climbs about 0.1–0.5° per hour, so a 0.3° horizon error shifts sunrise or sunset by roughly 1–3 h.
- Coordinates: curated sites are cited; sites flagged `verify` still need a primary-source check.
- Power: a simple 30 % sun-tracking vertical panel; no thermal, dust or degradation model.
